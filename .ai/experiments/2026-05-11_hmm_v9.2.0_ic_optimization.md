### 1. Hypothesis
The stubborly positive Regime IC in v9.1.0 is due to two factors: (1) an overly long 40-hour (10-bar) measurement window that captures post-crash V-recoveries, and (2) discrete/binary scoring that fails to provide enough "rank depth" for Spearman correlation. By shortening the window to 16 hours (4-bar) and overhauling the Crisis Detector with continuous Sigmoid-based scoring, we can shift the IC towards the negative target and redefine `CRISIS` as a preventive early-warning signal.

### 2. Implementation (v9.2.0)
- **IC Measurement**: Shortened forward window from 10-bar to **4-bar** in `test_universe_to_hmm.py`.
- **Continuous Scoring**:
    - **Rule 1 (Calm Before Storm)**: `v_low * expit(funding_z - 1.5)`. Focuses on structural overheating.
    - **Rule 2 (Structural Bearish)**: `v_high * d_bear`. Direct interaction of Layer 1 & 2 posteriors.
    - **Rule 3 (Price Shock)**: `expit(-ret_z - 2.5)`. Sigmoid-smoothed Z-score for price drops.
- **Decay & Suppression**:
    - **Liquidity Decay**: Multiplicative factor `1.0 - 0.7 * expit(liq_z - 3.0)`.
    - **Return Penalty**: Multiplicative `0.5x` during positive bars (preserves ranking better than subtraction).
- **Threshold**: Increased from 0.25 to **0.35** to account for continuous baseline noise.
