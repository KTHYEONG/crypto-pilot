### 1. Hypothesis
The v9.2.1 baseline had a narrow "CRISIS" definition limited to high volatility + bearish direction, resulting in a lagging signal and positive Regime IC (triggered at market bottoms). By (1) including the `vol_mid` transition phase in the crisis base, (2) suppressing overheating noise at price peaks via Rule 1 refinement, and (3) adjusting sticky durations, we can achieve a "Predictive Pivot" that captures the tail event *before* it fully materializes, thereby shifting IC to negative and maximizing Lead-Lag power.

### 2. Implementation (4-Step Evolution)
- **Step 1: Capitulation Safety (C)**: Raised bypass threshold from -2.5σ to -3.5σ. Prevents the model from misidentifying a true crash as a "washout" too early.
- **Step 2: Sticky Duration (B)**: Reduced BULL_CALM persistence (36→24) and increased CRISIS/BEAR persistence (1→3). Goal was to reduce blocking of crisis entries and ensure signal stability.
- **Step 3: Predictive Transition (A)**: Updated `crisis_base` to include `p_mid * p_bear` (Weight 0.8) alongside `p_high`. Expanded the "pre-crash" detection zone.
- **Step 4: Overheating Suppression (D)**: Refined `detect_crisis` Rule 1. Raised `v_low` funding threshold to 2.0 and added a `v_mid` funding trigger (1.5). Successfully decoupled "normal bull peaks" from "structural risk."
