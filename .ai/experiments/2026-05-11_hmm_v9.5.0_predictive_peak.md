### 1. Hypothesis
The v9.4.x iterations showed that `BEAR_TREND` was "stealing" high-quality crash signals from `CRISIS` because of overlapping semantic definitions. By isolating `BEAR_TREND` to `p_mid * p_bear` (stable transition) and mapping `p_high * p_bear` exclusively to `CRISIS` with high dominance (Weight 2.5, Clip 0.65), we can consolidate defensive energy into the `CRISIS` state, maximizing both **Lead-Lag predictive power** and **Tail Defense purity**.

### 2. Implementation (v9.5.0 Spec)
- **Step 1: Semantic Isolation (`hmm_inferrer.py`)**:
    - `bear_trend` = `p_mid * p_bear` (Removed `p_high` component).
    - `crisis_base` = `(p_high * p_bear * 2.5 + p_mid * p_bear * 0.6).clip(0.0, 0.65)`.
- **Step 2: Softened Bounce Penalty (`crisis_detector.py`)**:
    - Triggered at `ret_z > 2.0` (0.2x) and `ret > 0` (0.7x). Prevents premature exit from crisis during standard volatility but suppresses it during explosive V-recoveries.
