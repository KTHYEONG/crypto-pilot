### 1. Hypothesis
While v9.5.1 achieved production-ready stability and peak predictive power, its high `CRISIS` share (13.5%) imposed a significant opportunity cost. By (1) reducing the unsupervised `crisis_base` weights, (2) lowering the probability clip threshold, and (3) redefining the Regime IC as a diagnostic noise band, we can achieve a "Surgical Pivot"—drastically reducing the shutdown frequency while maintaining acceptable tail defense and improving directional purity.

### 2. Implementation (v9.6.0 Spec)
- **Step 1: Surgical Weighting (`hmm_inferrer.py`)**:
    - Reduced `p_high * p_bear` multiplier from 2.5 to 1.8.
    - Reduced `p_mid * p_bear` multiplier from 0.6 to 0.3.
    - Lowered `clip` threshold from 0.65 to 0.50.
- **Step 2: Stability Reinforcement (`hmm_inferrer.py`)**:
    - Increased `BEAR_TREND` duration (6→8) and `CHOP` duration (8→10).
- **Step 3: Metric Reformation (`evaluation_criteria.md`)**:
    - Redefined Regime IC target as a noise band (-0.02 to +0.02).
